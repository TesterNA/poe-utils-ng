How many Voyages your Charts actually make. A Voyage is nine Charts on a 3x3 board, and the only thing that has to line up is the Connections: each one leads off the board, or into a Connection facing it. Say how many Charts of each shape you have and the builder lays out as many complete boards as they are worth, each Chart spent once. Modifiers are not weighed here — this answers whether it fits.
